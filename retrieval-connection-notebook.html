Connection notebook

Recall an earlier idea and explain a meaningful connection.

How to run it

  1. Bring back an idea

    Recall a key idea from an earlier lesson without notes.

  2. Make a connection

    Link it to another taught idea or a familiar situation.

  3. Explain the link

    Write a short explanation showing how the connection works.

  4. Verify it

    Check the recalled knowledge and repair inaccurate links.

Prepare

A notebook; one connection prompt about taught knowledge.

Check learning

A shared word is not enough: ask what the relationship explains.

Adapt for your class

Provide two topic headings or let students record an explanation.

Credits & source

Agarwal attributes Connection Notebooks to James M. Lang. This starter adaptation recalls earlier taught knowledge and checks accuracy before extending a connection.
