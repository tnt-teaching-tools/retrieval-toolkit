Leitner flashcard boxes

Review difficult cards more often and secure cards after a longer gap.

How to run it

  1. Set a schedule

    Label piles frequent, next review, and later review; add dates.

  2. Retrieve before turning

    Answer each due card aloud or on paper before checking.

  3. Sort by accuracy

    Move correct cards forward; return missed cards to frequent.

  4. Return after a gap

    Review due piles on their dates, including previously secure cards.

Prepare

Checked question-and-answer cards; three labelled piles and review dates.

Check learning

Judge completeness against the checked answer, not a feeling of familiarity.

Adapt for your class

Use accessible digital piles or larger cards; keep the set manageable.

Credits & source

The Leitner system is attributed to Sebastian Leitner. This guide simplifies it to three review piles; intervals are teacher choices, not a fixed universal schedule.
