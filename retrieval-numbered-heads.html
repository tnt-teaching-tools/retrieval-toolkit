Numbered Heads Together

Everyone prepares an answer before a team member responds.

How to run it

  1. Number the team

    Assign numbers and pose a recall-and-explain prompt.

  2. Write alone

    Every student attempts an answer before discussion.

  3. Put heads together

    Compare reasoning so each member can explain the answer.

  4. Respond and check

    Call a number; that member answers, then all correct privately.

Prepare

Teams of three or four; a taught-content prompt; paper.

Check learning

Sample more than one team; revisit a corrected point independently.

Adapt for your class

Use response boards or written answers instead of standing.

Credits & source

Kagan describes formalising and naming the structure from Russ Frank’s classroom practice, then adding an individual written response. This retrieval adaptation uses that individual-first sequence.
