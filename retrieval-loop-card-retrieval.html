Loop-card retrieval

Recall an answer before locating its matching card.

How to run it

  1. Hide the answers

    Begin with the prompt side or cover visible answer panels.

  2. Answer first

    Each learner writes or says the answer from memory.

  3. Find the next link

    Reveal cards and locate the matching answer and next prompt.

  4. Check the chain

    Resolve wrong matches; retry a prompt without the cards.

Prepare

A teacher-checked loop of prompt-and-answer cards.

Check learning

Finding a printed match is the checking stage, not the recall attempt.

Adapt for your class

Use a short seated chain; larger text or digital cards.

Credits & source
