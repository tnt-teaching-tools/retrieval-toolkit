Quiz–Quiz–Trade

Partners retrieve, check, swap cards and repeat.

How to run it

  1. Pair up

    Give each learner a checked card; pair students safely.

  2. Quiz both ways

    One partner asks; the other answers from memory. Then reverse roles.

  3. Check together

    Use the card answer to correct or extend each response.

  4. Trade and repeat

    Exchange cards and work with a new partner, or rotate seated partners.

Prepare

Teacher-checked prompt cards with answers on the back

Check learning

Keep the answer hidden until an attempt. Sample explanations to catch inaccurate peer coaching.

Adapt for your class

Offer a seated rotation and read prompts aloud where helpful.

Credits & source

Quiz–Quiz–Trade is a Kagan structure. This is an independently written retrieval guide; no Kagan card sets or artwork are reproduced.
