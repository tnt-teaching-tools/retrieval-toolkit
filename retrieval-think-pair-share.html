Think–Pair–Share

Retrieve privately, compare with a partner, then explain to the class.

How to run it

  1. Think first

    Close notes and write your own answer before talking.

  2. Pair up

    Each partner explains their answer and one reason.

  3. Share reasoning

    Invite pairs to share a useful explanation or uncertainty.

  4. Check and retry

    Clarify the answer; everyone recalls the corrected idea alone.

Prepare

One prompt about previously taught content; paper or boards.

Check learning

Listen for reasoning, not simply agreement between partners.

Adapt for your class

Allow a written or recorded exchange in place of public speaking.

Credits & source

Think–Pair–Share is a shared classroom structure. This version follows Agarwal’s retrieval guidance; it does not claim she invented the original structure.
