Odd one out

Recall the properties that justify which item does not belong.

How to run it

  1. Inspect the set

    Show familiar items without their explanations.

  2. Recall properties

    Write what you remember about each relevant item.

  3. Justify a choice

    Identify an odd item using those recalled properties.

  4. Check the reasoning

    Compare with the taught knowledge; discuss valid alternatives.

Prepare

Three or four taught items with a defensible intended relationship.

Check learning

Accept another choice when its recalled evidence and rule are accurate.

Adapt for your class

Use pictures or two contrasting items before a larger set.

Credits & source

Campbell describes this approach in her classroom blog. The original inventor of the wider routine is not verified; this guide adapts it for any taught topic.
