How and why recall

Retrieve an explanation of how something works and why it happens.

How to run it

  1. Recall the essentials

    Individually recover the relevant taught facts.

  2. Explain how

    Describe the mechanism or sequence in your own words.

  3. Explain why

    Give the taught reasons that make the explanation work.

  4. Check the account

    Verify the explanation; repair invented or missing links.

Prepare

A familiar idea and teacher-checked how/why prompts.

Check learning

If prior knowledge is too thin, reteach instead of inviting unsupported guesses.

Adapt for your class

Use sentence stems or a simple sequence outline.
