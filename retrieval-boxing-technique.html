Boxing Technique

Hide key slide content and track what you can actually recall.

How to run it

  1. Box important content

    Cover selected information and add a short recall cue.

  2. Retrieve after a gap

    Return later and answer without moving the box.

  3. Reveal and evaluate

    Uncover and compare your response for accuracy and completeness.

  4. Label for next review

    Mark complete, partial or missed; revisit gaps after a delay.

Prepare

A checked slide or document with opaque boxes over key information.

Check learning

Labels follow checked performance, not confidence; keep the original hidden content.

Adapt for your class

Use paper covers; include text labels so colour is optional.

Credits & source

Culhane credits himself and his students with developing this technique in his guest blog. This classroom adaptation uses text labels alongside colour to track actual recall, rather than confidence alone.
