Retrieval role-play

Use recalled knowledge in a brief explanation or scenario.

How to run it

  1. Recall the essentials

    Individually list relevant knowledge with notes closed.

  2. Set the roles

    Choose a speaker and listener in a simple scenario.

  3. Use the knowledge

    Explain or demonstrate the recalled ideas in the scene.

  4. Check the content

    Verify the explanation; correct it before a second attempt.

Prepare

A short situation that draws on previously taught ideas.

Check learning

Evaluate accurate use of knowledge, not acting skill.

Adapt for your class

Keep roles seated; offer a written dialogue or audio response.

Credits & source

Hall Houston identifies this activity as his own in the linked TESOL blog. This subject-neutral adaptation adds individual thinking and teacher-checked feedback.
