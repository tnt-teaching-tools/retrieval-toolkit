Brain dump

Bring a whole topic back to mind on a blank page.

How to run it

  1. Set a boundary

    Name a previously taught topic and close notes.

  2. Recall independently

    Everyone writes or sketches what they remember for two minutes.

  3. Organise the recall

    Group related ideas and mark uncertain details.

  4. Check and improve

    Compare with a trusted reference; correct errors in another colour.

Prepare

Paper or mini whiteboards

Check learning

Sample explanations as well as keywords. Ask students to retrieve a corrected idea again later.

Adapt for your class

Use broad headings if a blank page feels overwhelming; allow spoken recording or labelled sketches.
