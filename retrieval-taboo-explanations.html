Taboo explanations

Explain a taught term without using the term itself.

How to run it

  1. Recall the meaning

    Both students privately recall the first term’s meaning.

  2. Explain without naming

    One partner describes its properties without saying its name.

  3. Identify and justify

    The listener names the term and gives a reason.

  4. Check then swap

    Verify the explanation and change roles for another term.

Prepare

Familiar term cards; a checked reference for definitions.

Check learning

Make accuracy the goal; avoid obscure banned-word rules that block explanation.

Adapt for your class

Allow a written clue or extra thinking time.

Credits & source
