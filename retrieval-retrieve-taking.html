Retrieve-taking

Pause the source and build notes from memory.

How to run it

  1. Study a short section

    Read or listen attentively; identify its main purpose.

  2. Hide the source

    Close the text or pause and hide the slide.

  3. Reconstruct the meaning

    Write the main ideas and relationships in your own words.

  4. Check the gaps

    Reopen the source, correct your notes, and revisit later.

Prepare

A short taught text, explanation or video; paper.

Check learning

Compare meaning and important omissions, not exact wording.

Adapt for your class

Use short sections, spoken notes or a labelled sketch.

Credits & source

Agarwal’s named classroom strategy changes note taking into recall with the source closed. Use older material too, so the task goes beyond immediate recall.
