Because, but, so

Recall knowledge and extend it into reasons, contrasts and consequences.

How to run it

  1. Recall the content

    Jot relevant facts or ideas before completing stems.

  2. Explain because

    Complete a stem with an accurate reason.

  3. Develop but and so

    Add a valid contrast and a justified consequence.

  4. Check the relationships

    Verify the facts and whether each connection follows.

Prepare

Three sentence stems grounded in taught content.

Check learning

Do not reward polished sentences that contain inaccurate knowledge.

Adapt for your class

Rehearse orally or start with one conjunction.

Credits & source

The because/but/so writing routine is associated with Hochman and Wexler’s The Writing Revolution. Bradford Research School describes adapting it for retrieval and elaboration. This guide follows that combined purpose.
