Walkabout bingo

Exchange explanations using a question grid.

How to run it

  1. Attempt privately

    Try the grid questions alone before meeting peers.

  2. Find a partner

    Take turns explaining an answer without opening notes.

  3. Record improvements

    Add useful explanations separately from your first attempt.

  4. Check then retry

    Teacher resolves gaps; repeat one question independently.

Prepare

A question grid with checked answers; individual first-attempt space.

Check learning

Everyone needs a recall attempt; copying a partner’s answer is feedback.

Adapt for your class

Use seated partner rotations and a smaller grid.

Credits & source

Houston attributes Walkabout Bingo to Jones’s Retrieval Practice book. This adaptation adds a private first attempt and a final individual recall, and removes the race to finish.

Kate Jones — published Walkabout Bingo; summarised by Hall Houston
