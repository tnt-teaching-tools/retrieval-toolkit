Low-stakes mini-quiz

A short individual quiz with immediate explanations.

How to run it

  1. Choose useful knowledge

    Select taught ideas, including one from longer ago.

  2. Everyone answers

    Give silent thinking time; keep notes closed.

  3. Explain the answers

    Reveal checked answers and address likely misconceptions.

  4. Retrieve a correction

    Hide one corrected answer and ask everyone to try again.

Prepare

Three to five teacher-written prompts and checked answers.

Check learning

Use errors to decide what needs another retrieval opportunity.

Adapt for your class

Read prompts aloud or reduce the number without removing thinking time.

Credits & source

Mini-quizzes are a longstanding teaching routine. Agarwal and Bain’s Powerful Teaching includes this approach; this guide credits their classroom guidance rather than claiming they invented quizzes.
