Cornell cue recall

Cover the notes and use a cue column to retrieve their meaning.

How to run it

  1. Prepare the cues

    Add brief questions beside existing, checked notes.

  2. Cover the notes

    Leave only the cue column visible.

  3. Recite the answers

    Explain each cue from memory, aloud or in writing.

  4. Check and schedule

    Uncover notes, correct gaps, and plan a later review.

Prepare

Previously prepared notes with a separate cue/question column.

Check learning

Read the cue, not the answer; check explanations against notes.

Adapt for your class

Provide teacher-written cues or use an audio response.

Credits & source

Cornell’s Learning Strategies Center credits Walter Pauk’s system. This activity focuses on its cover-and-recite review stage, rather than treating copying notes as retrieval.
