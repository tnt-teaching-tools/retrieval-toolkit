Elevator explanation

Compress a recalled idea into a clear short explanation.

How to run it

  1. Recall privately

    List the essential idea and one supporting detail.

  2. Choose the message

    Organise what a listener needs to understand.

  3. Explain briefly

    Give a short explanation without reading notes.

  4. Verify and improve

    Check omissions, then repeat an improved version.

Prepare

A taught topic and a partner or recording option.

Check learning

Do not let the time limit remove essential qualifications.

Adapt for your class

Offer a written version or more than thirty seconds.

Credits & source

Sprenger describes this classroom format. This adaptation makes the first attempt closed-note and adds checked feedback and a later revisit. The original inventor of the wider routine is not verified.
