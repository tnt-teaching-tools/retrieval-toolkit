Recall, sort and explain

Generate remembered examples before organising them.

How to run it

  1. Recall examples

    Individually generate examples from the taught topic.

  2. Sort them

    Place your recalled examples under suitable categories.

  3. Explain a boundary

    Describe why one example belongs in its category.

  4. Verify and retry

    Check accuracy; recall another example with notes closed.

Prepare

Taught category headings; paper; checked examples.

Check learning

Sorting visible answers alone is recognition; preserve the first recall stage.

Adapt for your class

Provide category headings or let students sort sketches.
