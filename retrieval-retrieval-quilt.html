Retrieval quilt

Build and explain connections between neighbouring ideas.

How to run it

  1. Recall each idea

    Individually jot the meaning of several grid terms.

  2. Choose neighbours

    Pick two adjacent ideas and identify a relationship.

  3. Explain the seam

    Write how and why the ideas connect.

  4. Check the joins

    Verify the recalled meanings and revise weak connections.

Prepare

A grid of familiar topic headings or terms.

Check learning

Require a meaningful relationship rather than two copied definitions.

Adapt for your class

Start with four terms; offer connector stems.

Credits & source

Campbell describes this approach in her classroom blog. The original inventor of the wider routine is not verified; this guide adapts it for any taught topic.
