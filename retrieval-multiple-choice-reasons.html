Multiple choice with reasons

Choose an answer and explain why alternatives do not fit.

How to run it

  1. Read the choices

    Use a question about previously taught knowledge.

  2. Commit privately

    Choose independently and write a short reason.

  3. Compare the reasoning

    Discuss why a tempting alternative is inaccurate.

  4. Check and transfer

    Clarify the answer, then ask a short related question.

Prepare

Teacher-written choices based on common misconceptions.

Check learning

Guessing a letter is not enough evidence of understanding.

Adapt for your class

Read choices aloud; use fewer well-designed alternatives.
