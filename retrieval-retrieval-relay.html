Retrieval relay

Take turns contributing to a checked team response.

How to run it

  1. Set the turns

    Give each team the same prompts and a clear turn order.

  2. Recall independently

    Everyone drafts an answer before the team discusses it.

  3. Pass the turn

    Each member contributes one answer or explanation in turn.

  4. Check and revisit

    Correct the team sheet; finish with one private reattempt.

Prepare

A small prompt set, team answer sheet and checked answers.

Check learning

Keep all students thinking between turns; do not reward speed alone.

Adapt for your class

Run it seated with a passed sheet; allow nonverbal contributions.

Credits & source

Jones includes Retrieval Relay in her Seneca CPD. This seated adaptation adds an individual response before each team turn and prioritises accuracy over speed.
