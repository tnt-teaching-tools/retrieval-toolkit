Cops and robbers

Recall alone, then discover what your peers remembered.

How to run it

  1. Recall alone

    Write remembered knowledge in the first column, with notes closed.

  2. Compare

    Read a partner’s recall and identify something you missed.

  3. Add separately

    Put new ideas in the second column to distinguish your recall from borrowed knowledge.

  4. Verify and retrieve

    Check accuracy, then cover both columns and recall a new idea.

Prepare

Paper split into “My recall” and “From peers”

Check learning

Peer additions are feedback, not independent retrieval. Verify them before the final recall.

Adapt for your class

Use fixed pairs instead of movement, and headings to support a narrower focus.
